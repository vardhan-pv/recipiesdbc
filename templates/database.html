Primary keysInteger IDs identify recipes and ingredients; the linking table uses a composite key.
Foreign keysEach recipe ingredient points to an existing recipe and ingredient. Deleting a recipe removes its links.
NormalizationIngredients are stored once and reused through a many-to-many relationship, reducing duplicate data.
ConstraintsRecipe names and ingredient names are unique. Quantities cannot be negative; servings must be positive.
